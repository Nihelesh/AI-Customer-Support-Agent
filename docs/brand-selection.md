## Selected Brand

**AmazonHelp**

## Selection Rationale

AmazonHelp was selected as the target brand for the AI customer-support agent.

The primary reasons are its high historical support volume, the highest number of reconstructed conversation threads among the evaluated candidates, and substantially richer conversations with an average of approximately 4.5 messages per reconstructed conversation.

AmazonHelp also demonstrated preliminary issue diversity comparable to AppleSupport, with 10 issue categories identified during the initial exploration.

The combination of conversation volume, conversation depth, and issue diversity makes AmazonHelp a suitable dataset for developing and evaluating the proposed customer-support AI system.

The conversation counts represent reconstructed response-chain threads from the dataset and should not be interpreted as confirmed real-world issue resolutions.
